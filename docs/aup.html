Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

Effective date: 26 July 2026 · Last updated: 26 July 2026 · Contact: epictechai@gmail.com

This AUP is mandatory. Violations may result in refusal of support, reporting to authorities where required, and—if you use any hosted services we later offer—suspension of access.

1. Purpose

Epic Tech AI software is powerful: it can run code, browse, call APIs, and generate content. This AUP defines uses that are not allowed so the project remains legitimate, safe, and lawful.

2. Illegal and harmful activities

You may not use the Software or Site to:

  • Violate any applicable law or regulation;
  • Engage in fraud, identity theft, social-engineering scams, or financial crime;
  • Traffic in illegal goods or services;
  • Exploit, abuse, or endanger children (including any sexual content involving minors);
  • Harass, threaten, stalk, or incite violence against people or groups;
  • Produce or disseminate defamatory content you know to be false with intent to harm.

3. Cybersecurity abuse

  • No developing, distributing, or operationalizing malware, ransomware, botnets, spyware, or exploits;
  • No unauthorized access to systems, networks, or data (hacking);
  • No denial-of-service attacks or intentional disruption of third-party services;
  • No bypassing security or authentication without explicit permission from the owner;
  • Security research must be authorized and responsible; report issues per SECURITY.md.

4. Privacy and data

  • Do not scrape, collect, or process personal data unlawfully;
  • Do not dox or publish others’ private information without lawful basis;
  • Do not put secrets, passwords, or regulated personal data into git-tracked memory files;
  • Comply with GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and other privacy laws when they apply to your use.

5. AI-specific misuse

  • No intentional generation of deceptive deepfakes for fraud or non-consensual intimate imagery;
  • No automated decision-making that unlawfully discriminates in protected contexts;
  • No claiming AI output is human professional advice (legal, medical, financial) without proper disclaimers and qualified review;
  • No using agents to spam, mass-phish, or manipulate electoral processes illegally.

6. Payments and commerce

  • Do not use agents to create surprise charges or unauthorized payments;
  • Stripe and similar tools must follow provider terms and human-approved pricing/Checkout flows;
  • You are responsible for tax, consumer, and financial regulations in your jurisdiction.

7. Infrastructure and fair use

  • Do not abuse third-party APIs beyond rate limits or terms;
  • Do not use the project branding to impersonate Epic Tech AI or misrepresent affiliation;
  • Do not overload shared resources in a way that harms others.

8. Open-source compliance

Respect the MIT License and third-party licenses of dependencies and model providers. Do not remove copyright notices.

9. Reporting

Report abuse or security issues to epictechai@gmail.com.

10. Changes

We may update this AUP by posting a new version on the Site.
