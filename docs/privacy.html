Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 July 2026 · Last updated: 26 July 2026 · Contact: epictechai@gmail.com

This Policy explains how Epic Tech AI handles information in connection with https://epictechai.github.io/epic-tech-ai-agent/ and the open-source repository. The GitHub Pages site is a static documentation/launcher site and is designed to collect little or no personal data.

1. Who we are

Epic Tech AI (“we”) maintains the open-source super-agent project. Contact: epictechai@gmail.com · https://github.com/epictechai · https://x.com/EpicTechAI

2. Information we collect

2.1 Website (GitHub Pages)

  • We do not operate user accounts on the static site.
  • We do not intentionally collect names, emails, or payment data via the static pages.
  • Hosting logs: GitHub (Pages) may process standard technical data (IP address, user agent, request URL, timestamps) under GitHub’s privacy policy. We do not control GitHub’s infrastructure.
  • Local browser features: the launcher page may use the Clipboard API when you click “Copy” (only with your action). We do not upload clipboard contents to our servers.
  • No first-party advertising cookies are set by our static pages as shipped.

2.2 Software you run locally or on your infrastructure

When you run the agent on your machine or cloud:

  • Prompts, files, logs, and tool outputs stay in your environment unless you send them to third parties.
  • If you configure model providers (e.g. via OpenCode / API keys), those providers process prompts and outputs under their privacy policies.
  • If you connect SaaS tools (GitHub, Stripe, Slack, etc.), those processors receive data you authorize.
  • Project memory files under .kortix/memory/ are stored where you run the project (often in git). Do not put secrets or sensitive personal data there.

2.3 Communications

If you email us (e.g. security reports), we process the content of your message and your email address to respond.

3. How we use information

  • To operate and improve documentation and the open-source project;
  • To respond to inquiries and security reports;
  • To comply with law and enforce our Terms / AUP.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK where applicable)

Where GDPR/UK GDPR applies, we rely on legitimate interests (running an open-source project and site), contract (if you engage us), and consent (where required), and legal obligation.

5. Sharing

We may share information with:

7. Retention

Email correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and maintain project records. Hosting logs follow the host’s retention. Local software data retention is controlled by you.

8. Security

See SECURITY.md. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Protect your API keys; never commit secrets.
